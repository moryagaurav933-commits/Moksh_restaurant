OUR STORY

Moksha is more than a cafe — it's a gathering place in the hills where neighbors and travelers find a table. Born from a love of honest cooking and quiet views, our kitchen turns seasonal produce into shared plates and memorable moments.

We believe dining should be unhurried. The menu changes with the season, highlighting local farms and traditional Himachali flavors reinterpreted with a light, modern touch. Our team strives to serve familiar comforts and small surprises with equal warmth.

Whether you're here for morning coffee, a late lunch, or an evening dinner, expect generous portions, mindful sourcing, and a relaxed pace that lets the hills do the rest.

Meet the Team

  • Chef Aman — head chef and co-founder. Grew up in the region and crafts menus around seasonal produce and local techniques.
  • Priya — front-of-house and events coordinator, curates the dining room energy and private gatherings.
  • Rishi — barista and beverage lead, responsible for our chai program and small-batch juices.
